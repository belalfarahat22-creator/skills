# Etisalat Red

A crisp red-and-white corporate theme in the style of the etisalat (UAE) brand: mostly white, deep ink text, one bold red accent.

## Color Palette

- **Etisalat Red**: `#E00800` - The single accent: key figures, buttons, highlight bars
- **Ink**: `#140F21` - Text, titles and dark section slides
- **White**: `#ffffff` - Main background
- **Soft Grey**: `#F4F3F6` - Panels and alternate rows

## Typography

- **Headers**: Inter Bold (Arial Bold where Inter isn't installed)
- **Body Text**: Inter (Arial)

## Best Used For

Telecom and retail store reports, team briefings, sales dashboards, internal etisalat material. For the full rules (Arabic, logo, status colours, audit script) use the `etisalat-brand` skill.
